# Signal post — for the group

Time for another in the series of apps I've spent more time using than making:  

  Speak, Memory

  https://summarizing-journal.vercel.app/
  
I'm ripping off the title of Vladimir Nabokov's memoire because it is so perfect for this app.  Other possible names:  Suma Vida, Facts of Life, As the World Turns.

Here is what Claude said about it.

    The trick that makes it actually usable: you talk (or type), but it never shows you back your own words. The moment you finish, it summarizes what you said into a whole ladder — an outline, a complete write-up, a paragraph, a sentence, a phrase, even a single word — and *that's* what it displays. So you can be a total mess when you put things in: ramble, hem and haw, backtrack, say something ungrammatical, switch topics halfway through a sentence. None of it shows. (Your raw words are still saved — you can pull them up anytime — they're just not the thing you look at.) Weirdly, knowing you won't be confronted with your own dictation makes it so much easier to just say the thing.

    The other idea is that it's summaries all the way up *and* down. Every entry gets those levels, but so does every week, month, year, decade — and the categories you file memories under (people, places, whatever), on up to a single node for your whole life. Every node summarizes its children into the exact same levels: outline, prose summary, paragraph, sentence, phrase, word. So you can stand at "your whole life," see the one word and one sentence it came up with, then zoom down — decade, year, day — until you hit your actual words. It even draws the whole thing as a little graph you can watch fill in.

    It runs in your phone's browser, everything stays on your device, no accounts, nothing posted anywhere. I'd love a few real people poking at it. Try this: say one honest thing about today, then dredge up one old memory, and go look at what it makes of them. 

My life word is "tangle" because I just told it my romantic history. Tell me what's confusing or broken.
