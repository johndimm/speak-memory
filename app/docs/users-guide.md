# Speak, Memory — User's Guide

A phone-first journal that **summarizes everything you write** — compressing it *upward* into ever-shorter forms: the full text, a complete summary, a paragraph, a single **sentence**, a short **phrase**, and finally one **word**. It does this at every scale of time, from a single day to your whole life. When you read, you travel the ladder the other way — start from the word and **zoom down** into the detail, all the way to your exact words.

Everything lives **on your device** (in the browser). Summaries are generated by an AI model; your words are the source of truth.

---

## The summarization ladder

When you write, the app summarizes your words **upward** — each rung a tighter distillation than the one below it:

> full text → complete summary → paragraph → **sentence** → **phrase** → **word**

And it doesn't stop at one day. Days roll up into a week, weeks into a month, and on up through years and decades to your whole **life** — so you can ask the same question at any scale: *what was this, in a word?*

The fun — and the point — is at the top. Your whole life gets a single **word**. Obviously no word can hold a life; take it as a wink, a little more serious than a fortune cookie. The **sentence** is where it gets interesting: one sentence for a life can land on something essential about you. The word is play; the sentence can be true.

Reading works in reverse: every page leads with the word, the phrase, the sentence — and you open the fuller summary, the outline, or your verbatim words only when you want to go deeper.

---

## The four tabs

- **Write** — put things in.
- **Journal** — read things back, at any zoom.
- **Graph** — see the whole structure and watch it get summarized.
- **⚙ (Settings)** — voice, data, and this guide.

---

## Writing

### A journal entry (today, or any day)
Open **Write**. The date defaults to today. Type or tap **🎤 Dictate**, add photos if you like, and **Save**. That's it — no headline to invent. The AI writes the one-word / phrase / sentence summaries for you in the background.

To back-date a plain entry, just change the **Date**.

### A memory (something from the past)
Tap **"＋ A past memory?"**. Give it:
- a **Category** (childhood, schools, girlfriends…),
- an optional **Subject** (a name — a person, a place, a thing),
- a **Year** or **year range**, or check **"still going"** for something ongoing.

Then write it and Save. Under the hood a memory is just a **back-dated journal entry that also has a category and a subject** — the app treats them the same everywhere.

**One per anchor, edit to add.** There's one entry per day and one memory per subject; to add more, you edit the existing one. From any memory page you can tap **"Add another to …"** to start a new one with the category and subject already filled in.

## Reading in the Journal

Every page — a day, a week, a decade, a category, your whole Life — follows the **same shape**:

- the **name** of where you are,
- a **one-word** summary, a **phrase**, and a **sentence**,
- **Complete summary** — tap it and the sentence *zooms* into the full multi-paragraph summary in place,
- the **list of what's inside** (a year lists its months; a category lists its people),
- an **Outline**,
- and, on a day or a memory, the **Verbatim transcript** — your exact words.

Drill down by tapping the cards; the **breadcrumb** at the top walks you back up.

Memories also get a **timeline** on their category and subject pages — a bar per memory across the years, with the name beside it. Tap a bar to jump to that memory.

### "The summary isn't right?"
On any day or memory, open **"The summary isn't right?"**, say what's wrong (a misspelled name, a wrong year, two things mixed up), and tap **Fix the summary**. It re-summarizes with your note — and **remembers the note**, so future versions keep honoring it.

## The Graph

The **Graph** tab shows the whole thing as a living diagram: **Life** at the top, then decades and categories, years and subjects, months, weeks, and finally days and memories at the bottom. Lines connect each parent to the children it summarizes.

- **Clean** nodes sit quiet; **dirty** ones glow; the one being **summarized right now** pulses. Watch the glow climb from a new entry all the way up to Life.
- **Tap a node** for a quick preview — its name and one-line summary — with **Open ›** to jump to that page in the Journal.
- **Pinch / drag / +– / Reset** to explore; **⤢** goes full screen. Rotate to landscape for more room.

---

## Settings

- **Journal → Opens on** — where the Journal starts (week, month, year, decade, or your whole life).
- **Summaries → Voice** — the author's voice used for all generated prose (clean editorial by default, or Hemingway, Didion, Chandler… or a custom one). Changing it re-summarizes in the background.
- **Your data** — **Export** everything to a file, **Import** a file back, or **Delete everything**. Export/import cover both entries and memories.

---

## Good to know

- **Speed:** a new entry's word/phrase/sentence appear in a couple of seconds. The heavy *complete summary* and *outline* are written only when you open them.
- **Privacy:** your journal is stored locally in your browser. Only the text being summarized is sent to the AI service; nothing is stored on a server by this app.
- **Rename it:** tap the ✎ next to the title to call your journal whatever you like.
